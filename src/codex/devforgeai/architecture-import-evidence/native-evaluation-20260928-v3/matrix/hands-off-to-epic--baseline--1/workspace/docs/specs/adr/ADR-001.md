# ADR-001 — Identity provider for volunteer sign-in

## Context and status

[PRD-001](../prd/PRD-001.md) explicitly calls for this ADR for FR-001. The supplied evidence does not identify a current identity tenant, supported volunteer sign-in channel, enrollment/recovery process, or operating budget. No provider has been selected or provisioned. This ADR is **proposed**, and FR-001 is **blocked for epic readiness** until a concrete choice is supported. Proceeding without questions means recording the missing evidence, not inventing a decision or stakeholder approval.

## Proposed direction and alternatives

Use a hosted identity service through an adapter that resolves to a stable local account ID. The application controls roles and revocable sessions as defined in [ARC-001](../architecture/ARC-001.md). Phone contact data is not an identity attribute exposed to volunteers or administrator-only users.

| Alternative | Potential fit | Evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Existing organization-managed hosted identity | Reuse established account administration if volunteers can be enrolled. | Whether a tenant exists, volunteer eligibility, account access/recovery, integration support, and cost. |
| Dedicated hosted identity service | Independent volunteer account lifecycle. | Usable sign-in channels for this population, enrollment/recovery, adapter support, administration, and cost. |
| Application-managed credentials on hosted infrastructure | Full control over credentials and recovery. | Additional security/operational ownership would be necessary; not the proposed direction for this small pilot. |

These are design alternatives, not verified vendor comparisons. No claims about current vendor capabilities or pricing are made.

## Resolution criteria

The delivery team should collect the following evidence, with Priya Nair as the proposed product contact (not a claim of assignment acceptance):

1. Identify the concrete hosted service and sign-in method, with evidence that the pilot volunteers can use it. Specify enrollment, duplicate account handling, account recovery, and privileged-account bootstrap.
2. Document adapter behavior for validated identity, stable subject mapping, login correlation, callback replay protection, sign-out, and fresh authentication following session revocation. Do not rely on provider token expiry for the five-minute application-session bound.
3. Demonstrate that provider credentials alone cannot call protected application APIs or silently exchange an old application session for a new one. Cover sign-in/revocation races and define which fresh sign-ins are allowed; account suspension would require a separate product requirement.
4. Verify hosted operation, server-held secrets, administrative access separation, and that volunteer phone contact data is not disclosed by the selected identity flow or metadata.
5. Record the choice, rejected alternatives, service ownership and cost assumptions, and integration evidence here; change status to accepted only when the decision is actually made. Update ARC-001's FR-001 readiness row and recheck impacted NFRs.

## Consequences and scope of the block

The unresolved provider blocks FR-001. FR-002 and FR-003 can be decomposed against the local principal interface; their integrated delivery still depends on sign-in. Shared application-session revocation, privacy boundaries, and hosted deployment constraints are defined independently, so NFR-001, NFR-002, and NFR-003 are ready to attach to those epics. Provider-specific integration verification remains outstanding. No approved PRD requirement is removed or weakened by this ADR.
