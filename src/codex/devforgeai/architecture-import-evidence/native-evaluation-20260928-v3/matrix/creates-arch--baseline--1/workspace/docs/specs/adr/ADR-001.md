# ADR-001 — Identity provider for volunteer sign-in

## Context

[PRD-001](../prd/PRD-001.md) explicitly leaves the identity provider as a needed ADR affecting FR-001. Volunteers must sign in from mobile browsers; administrators must revoke signed-in sessions within five minutes. The source does not establish an existing identity tenant, volunteer email/SMS availability, account provisioning/recovery policy, budget or provider access.

## Decision status

**Proposed; no provider selected.** Define a hosted identity adapter with immutable issuer/subject mapping and server-side proof validation. Use the local application sessions specified in [ARCH-001](../architecture/ARCH-001.md) and [ADR-002](ADR-002.md). Provider tokens cannot directly authorize product APIs.

This is a bounded unresolved decision, not an approved vendor choice. FR-001 and integrated NFR-001 are blocked for epic baselining; FR-002/FR-003 may be decomposed against the adapter/session contract, but cannot complete end-to-end acceptance until this decision is resolved.

## Alternatives to evaluate

| Option | Benefit | Unknown or cost |
|---|---|---|
| Existing hosted organizational identity service | May reuse account administration and existing support | Whether a tenant exists and external volunteers can use it is unknown |
| Dedicated managed identity service | Provides a hosted sign-in and account lifecycle boundary | Validate supported volunteer sign-in method, recovery, cost and operator access |
| Application-owned credentials | Full local control | Adds password/recovery security and operational work; not preferred for this small pilot |

No vendor-specific capabilities, prices or suitability have been verified. Vendor selection requires current primary documentation and a small integration check, not an assumed feature list.

## Resolution evidence required

The proposed technical lead, with Priya Nair supplying product constraints, should record:

1. Selected provider, sign-in method, tenant/account ownership, provisioning and recovery process, and volunteer access assumptions. Do not assume all volunteers have email or consent to an SMS workflow.
2. Validated callback/proof checks, issuer/subject mapping and local session creation, including malformed or replayed proofs and unauthorized account linking.
3. A revocation experiment with multiple sessions proving the local generation check rejects previous sessions within 300 seconds of successful revocation. Check provider refresh, silent login and recovery cannot silently resurrect a revoked local session.
4. Hosting compatibility, restricted secrets, and a phone-claim policy consistent with NFR-002. Do not place phone numbers in browser-visible identity claims or tokens.
5. Alternatives considered, current provider evidence, remaining risks, and the decision date. Update this ADR's status and ARCH-001's readiness table when the evidence supports resolution.

Proceeding without questions records the missing evidence; it does not fabricate the food bank's identity arrangements.
