# ADR-001 — Hosted volunteer identity provider and sign-in method

## Context

[PRD-001](../prd/PRD-001.md) explicitly identifies identity-provider selection as an unresolved ADR affecting FR-001. It establishes mobile-browser users but gives no volunteer email availability, existing identity tenant, account-enrollment source, recovery process, service budget, or operational account owner. A smartphone alone does not establish a usable login channel.

The identity integration must support application sessions that administrators can revoke within five minutes, avoid exposing volunteer phone numbers to non-coordinator product roles, and run on hosted services. The application session boundary is specified in [ARC-001](../architecture/ARC-001.md).

## Proposed direction and options

Use a managed hosted identity service behind an application-owned adapter, with internal principal IDs and authoritative application sessions. **No provider or sign-in method is selected in this ADR.** This direction permits provider-independent booking and roster design but does not close the PRD's open question.

| Option | Benefit to investigate | Material unknown or cost |
|---|---|---|
| Existing hosted organizational identity service | Could reuse existing accounts and operational ownership | No evidence that volunteers have accounts or that an existing tenant permits volunteer enrollment |
| Hosted customer identity service using verified email sign-in | Could avoid requiring organizational accounts | Email availability, delivery, recovery, enrollment controls, provider capability and price must be established |
| Application-built credentials | Direct control over account behavior | Adds password handling, abuse prevention and recovery responsibilities unsupported by the pilot's stated needs; do not choose by default |

These are option classes, not verified vendor recommendations. Phone-based login is not a default: the PRD only authorizes coordinator-visible phone data and does not establish a phone-authentication flow.

## Resolution criteria

Before marking this ADR accepted and FR-001 ready for full epic decomposition, record:

1. The selected hosted provider and sign-in method, with current first-party documentation supporting the needed integration behavior and service constraints.
2. Evidence that pilot volunteers can use that identity channel, plus the enrollment, duplicate-account mapping and recovery approach.
3. Named operational ownership and a feasible service plan; a controlled way to bootstrap coordinator and administrator roles.
4. A concrete provider-to-application authentication contract, including verification, browser-flow binding, and how revoked application sessions remain unusable despite valid provider sessions or tokens.
5. Defined sign-in and revocation integration checks, with actual provider proof required before release. Preserve the 300-second revocation ceiling and coordinator-only phone access.

Priya Nair is the PRD owner and the proposed resolver of missing product/operational inputs; no review or acceptance by her is asserted. No question or approval request is needed to preserve this blocker in the architecture.

## Consequences and readiness impact

- FR-001 is **blocked for full epic decomposition** until this decision is resolved.
- FR-002 and FR-003 are **ready for provider-independent epic decomposition** against ARC-001's session and role contracts; their production integration and release depend on FR-001.
- NFR-001 can be decomposed around application session revocation now, but full proof must include the real provider. NFR-002 and NFR-003 remain constraints on every identity option.
- The pilot cannot ship while this ADR remains unresolved. Do not label a test adapter as production authentication or treat this proposed ADR as accepted.
