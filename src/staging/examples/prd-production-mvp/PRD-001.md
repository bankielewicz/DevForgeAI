# PRD-001 — Online appointment booking

## 1. Summary

Patients book, move and cancel physiotherapy appointments online, and get a text reminder the day
before. This is the clinic's MVP, but real patients use it with real data from launch.

## 2. Problem and opportunity

Booking is phone-only during opening hours, so calls queue and patients give up (BRN-001#PRB-01).
Forgotten appointments lose slots (BRN-001#PRB-02).

## 3. Users and personas

Patients booking for themselves; front-desk staff managing the calendar.

## 4. Goals and non-goals

**Goals**
- Patients can manage their own appointments at any time.

**Non-goals**
- A native mobile app (rejected in BRN-001).
- A waitlist for cancelled slots (parked in BRN-001).
