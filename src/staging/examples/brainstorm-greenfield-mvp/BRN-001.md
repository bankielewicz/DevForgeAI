# BRN-001 — A booking service for a mobile bike repair van

## 1. Context

Project kind: greenfield

Maya Okafor repairs commuters' bikes from a van that parks at a different stop each weekday morning. Bookings
come by phone while she works, and many callers give up before a slot is agreed. She wants a booking service
for the van. No such service exists yet: today there is the phone, a paper diary and a job log kept in a
spreadsheet.

## 3. Target users

| Persona | Job to be done | Current workaround | Pain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bike commuter | Get a bike repaired without losing a working day | Phones the van, or leaves the bike at a shop for several days | Doesn't know where the van is, and can arrive to find the part missing |
| Van mechanic | Finish each repair without stopping to take bookings | Answers the phone between jobs and writes slots in a paper diary | About nine calls a day break off a repair |

## 5. Evaluation method
