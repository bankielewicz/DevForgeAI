# Completion summary

Load only at completion, transfer or recovery, after saving the detailed [handoff](handoff.md) and specification/change records. Replace illustrative locations with links to actual retained files. Never report these examples as observations.

The terminal display contains only:
- Actual outcome, scoped to what finished.
- Brief useful result.
- Primary artifact and handoff links.
- Material decision or blocker, including evidence limits.
- One immediate next action and its owner.

Aim for roughly 60–120 words when practical. Failures and necessary limits take precedence over brevity. Hashes, full inventories, checks, reasoning and history belong in saved artifacts. A short display cannot replace them or label a prepared handoff as executed. No special runtime receipt or new outbox is required.
