## What this revision changes (XSPEC-004-S1)

Revision 1 of this record, pinned in `upstream`, remains the authoring derivation for the
capability. This revision changes nothing about the selected requirements or their acceptance
meaning. It records one bounded correction pass resolving the six findings in CIR-003 of the
pinned integration review, and it revises only the sections those findings touch:

- the peer-source boundary, which revision 1 stated without qualifying it by role and read
  authority (CCTX-IR-001);
- the CCX-04 case set, which lacked the positive target-bootstrap contrast (CCTX-IR-002);
- the two checking helpers, whose claimed scopes exceeded what they establish
  (CCTX-IR-003, CCTX-IR-004);
- two record-format corrections (CCTX-IR-005, CCTX-IR-006).

CCR-001 through CCR-008, the case catalogue's required observations and material failures, the
selected specification and evaluation plan, the shared handoff template bytes, the recorded
baseline facts and every historical result stay as they were. No finding is resolved by
relaxing a criterion.

## Helper scope after correction (XSPEC-004-S6)

Helpers are optional under the governing stage instructions. Both are retained, with claims
narrowed to what they establish. Neither is evidence of native behavior, and neither may be
reported as semantic acceptance.

**Runtime helper, `scripts/check_receipt.py`.** Two separate modes, because they establish
different things:

| Mode | Establishes | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receipt verification, `--expected-sha256` | The claimed digest is 64 lowercase hex and recomputing the file's bytes reproduces it | Anything about the content's correctness, authority or acceptance |
| Self-receipt inspection, `--self-receipt-inspection` | PASS only when the file contains no 64-hex token at all, so absence of a self-receipt is established by absence of any digest. FAIL when the file literally contains its own final digest | Absence of a self-receipt in any other case. Every digest occurrence it cannot adjudicate is reported as COULD_NOT_RUN with its line, for separate inspection |

The literal own-digest predicate cannot be made to fail by construction, since a SHA-256 fixed
point is not reachable; it is retained and labelled non-discriminating in the script, its output
and the workflow prose rather than counted as a tested guarantee. A same-basename reference at a
different path is a legitimate historical reference and is no longer rejected. A multiline
self-receipt is no longer silently accepted; it is reported for inspection. The governing rule
that a finalized handoff must carry no receipt for itself is unchanged and is upheld by the
documented write order, not by a helper guarantee.

**Author-only checker.** A new output in the revising run, not a modification of the frozen
original. It normalizes each contained relative path once and classifies the normalized value, so
that alias spellings of one held-out file cannot receive different visibility dispositions. Path
containment obligations are unchanged.
