The finding had two parts: the helper falsely rejected a legitimate same-basename reference at a
different path, and it accepted a self-receipt split across lines while its documentation claimed
it established self-receipt absence. The correction narrows the helper. Receipt verification stays
a guarantee; self-receipt handling becomes an explicitly labelled inspection that reports what it
cannot adjudicate instead of passing or failing it.

neither rejects the legitimate case nor passes the multiline case. It reports both for the author
to read. The one case where absence IS established, no digest present at all, passes.

The literal own-digest predicate is retained and labelled non-discriminating. This suite does not
claim every sub-check has a constructible failing case: S5 records the one that does not, and why.

The governing rule that a finalized handoff carries no receipt for itself is unchanged. It is
upheld by the documented write order and the author's inspection of the listed lines, not by a
helper guarantee. No exit code here is semantic acceptance.
