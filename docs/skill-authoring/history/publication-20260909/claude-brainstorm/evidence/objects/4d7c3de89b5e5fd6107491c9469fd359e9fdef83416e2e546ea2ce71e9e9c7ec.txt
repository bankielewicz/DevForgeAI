## The shape of the R10 findings

Fourteen checker/control records, and several are the same mistake in different places: a
case that produces its expected outcome for a reason unrelated to what it tests. A
negative failing because its target is missing; a positive that cannot resolve its own
preflight path; a decoy rejected by a length cap rather than by the association rule; a
STALE assertion reading a gate file that never exists. Most of this run is making the
expected outcome arrive by the intended route — staging real prerequisite bytes, then
asserting the predicate, association or classification rather than the outcome alone.

Two repairs are worth naming because the defect was narrower than it looked. The
disclosure that "still passes as observed section absence" did so because `_UNKNOWN`
matched "unreadable" and "could not read" but not **"unable to read"**, so that exact
phrasing reached the absence branch. And the digest decoy worked *because* the wrong-root
locator was correctly rejected — rejecting it is what left its digest unowned for the
valid locator above to take.
