# Change request: cover every club trail and add photo reports

## Requested change

Revisit IDEA-001. The conditions feed should cover all eight trails the club
uses, and a report should be able to carry a photo.

## Reason

Members said a single-trail feed did not answer the question they actually had
on a Saturday morning, and that a photo settles "is it flooded" faster than a
sentence does.

## Scope

- In scope: IDEA-001, widened from one trail to all eight.
- Also in scope: photo attachment on a condition report.
- Out of scope: IDEA-002 route signups. Unchanged by this request.
