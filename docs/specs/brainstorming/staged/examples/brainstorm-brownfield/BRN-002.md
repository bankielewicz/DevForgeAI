# BRN-002 — Cutting missed visits among patients who book online

## 1. Context

Project kind: brownfield

Patients now book and move their own visits online, and a text reminder goes out the day before (PRD-001 v3).
Even so, 12% of the visits booked in October 2026 were missed. The clinic's practice manager wants ideas that
cut that rate without charging patients and without more work for the front desk.

## 5. Evaluation method

Improve existing: the product already exists, so the problems were taken from evidence about how it works today,
and ideas came from SCAMPER's seven prompts (substitute, combine, adapt, modify, put to another use, eliminate,
reverse) applied to it. Each idea was rated high, medium or low for value, effort and risk, with effort and risk
counted on top of what exists. The score is 2 × value − effort − risk, counting high = 3, medium = 2 and low = 1
(range −4 to 4); a higher score is a stronger candidate for promotion.

The open assumptions are ranked riskiest first: by what breaks among the first-release candidates if the
assumption is wrong (Fatal, Serious or Minor), then by how little is known (low confidence first), then by
assumption number. The first release cut tests the riskiest assumptions with at most three ideas.

Suggestions that PRD-001 already covers, and ideas that BRN-001 already decided, are listed in section 6 and are
not ideas here.
