# PRD-001 — Online booking for returning patients

## 1. Summary

Returning patients book, move and cancel their own visits online, sign in with a link sent to their e-mail
address, and get a text reminder the day before. Real patients use it with real data from launch.

## 2. Problem and opportunity

Booking is phone-only during opening hours, so calls queue and patients give up (BRN-001#PRB-01). Forgotten
visits lose slots (BRN-001#PRB-02).

## 3. Users and personas

Returning patients booking for themselves, and the front-desk staff who manage the calendar.

## 4. Goals and non-goals

**Goals**
- Returning patients can manage their own visits at any time of day.

**Non-goals**
- Booking by voice assistant (parked in the brainstorm).
- Charging a deposit at booking (rejected in the brainstorm).
