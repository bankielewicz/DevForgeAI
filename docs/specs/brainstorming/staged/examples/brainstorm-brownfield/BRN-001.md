# BRN-001 — Online appointment booking for returning patients

## 1. Context

Front-desk staff spend most mornings on the phone booking and moving visits, and some patients give up before
they get through. The clinic wants returning patients to book for themselves. This brainstorm was written
before the booking pages existed.

## 5. Evaluation method

Diverge-converge: ideas were generated without judgment, then rated high, medium or low for value, effort and
risk. The score is 2 × value − effort − risk, counting high = 3, medium = 2 and low = 1.

## 6. Convergence

Self-booking, the e-mail sign-in link and the day-before text were promoted. The waiting list and the voice
assistant were parked. The deposit was rejected. Dana confirmed these dispositions and that the brainstorm
has converged.
